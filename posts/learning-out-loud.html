Learning Out Loud: Why I Write About What I Don't Know

September 2026 · Mindset

Most tutorials pretend the author has always known everything. I haven't. I learn by breaking things and reading error messages until they make sense.

This blog is my place to record those lessons — honestly, without pretending I have it all figured out. If you're learning too, I hope these notes save you a browser tab or two of searching.
