Between Monday night and this morning, the site changed more than in the weeks before. Three things stand out.

A design experiment — a "notebook" theme — that I liked for an evening and reverted the same night. Not every idea needs to ship.

A full Chinese version of the site, with a language switcher in the nav. The biggest single addition so far.

And an honesty pass. The old copy said things like "every line is mine." opencode writes the code; I suggest, review, and decide. The site now says it plainly, starting with the title: "Be true to yourself."
