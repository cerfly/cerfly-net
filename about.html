About Me

👤 Who I Am

Hi, I'm cerfly — a self-taught developer. No CS degree, no bootcamp; just curiosity, a Linux Mint machine, and the stubborn hunch that I can learn just about anything, one commit at a time.

💻 How I Learn

I learn by building. I picked vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript because I wanted to understand the web from the ground up — no frameworks hiding the details. This site is my first real project. opencode — an AI assistant that doubles as a patient tutor and rubber duck in my terminal — writes the code; I decide what gets built and read every line before it ships.

My mantra is simple: build something, break something, fix it, then write down what I learned so I don't forget.

🌱 Beyond Code

When the terminal closes, you'll find me exploring the outdoors, listening to music, or tinkering with something unrelated to computers. A curious mind doesn't clock out easily.
