About Me

👤 Introduction

Hi, I'm Cerfly. I'm a curious developer based in Linux Mint land, exploring the world of programming, web development, and open-source tools.

The username "cerfly" comes from my love of tinkering, flying around different corners of tech, and making things work.

📖 My Journey

What started as curiosity about how computers work quickly grew into a passion for building things. I love the moment when a piece of code finally clicks, when a stubborn bug gives up, and when a project comes together.

I believe in learning by doing and sharing that knowledge with others, which is why I chose to build this site from the ground up.

🌱 Beyond Code

When I'm not at the keyboard, I enjoy exploring new places, listening to music, and picking up random skills just for fun. I think a well-rounded life makes for a better developer.
